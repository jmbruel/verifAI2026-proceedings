# Preface

## Event Information

This volume contains the post-workshop proceedings of **VERIF-AI 2026**, the First International Workshop on the Interplay between Artificial Intelligence and Software Verification, held on **March 8–11, 2026**, at the **LASER Conference Center in Villebrumier, France** (near Toulouse and Montauban), and organized by the **LASER Foundation**. 

## Scope and Aims

Artificial intelligence has rapidly transformed software construction, yet AI-guided development—often referred to as "vibe coding"—continues to face fundamental reliability and correctness challenges, most notably the phenomenon of hallucinations. VERIF-AI 2026 was established to bring together researchers and practitioners from artificial intelligence, formal methods, and software engineering to examine both directions of the interplay between AI and software verification: how AI tools themselves can be verified and, centrally, how advanced verification and validation ("V&V") techniques—ranging from formal proofs and static analysis to automated testing—can be combined with AI to make AI-assisted software development efficient, reliable, and trustworthy.

Rather than following a traditional conference format, VERIF-AI 2026 was designed as an intensive, discussion-oriented working forum where selected participants presented and debated ongoing advances. The workshop covered a broad spectrum of topics, including using verification and validation techniques to filter, control, and certify AI-produced software; leveraging AI to generate and validate specifications, implementations, proofs, static analyses, and test suites; assessing the quality of AI-generated software and vibe coding; addressing software hallucinations; applying AI to non-coding software engineering activities such as requirements, specifications, contracts, design, and testing; and empirical studies and experience reports on AI-supported software engineering.
