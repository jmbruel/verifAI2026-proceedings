Information for Volume Editors and Conference Organizers

Attached to this email you will find the Instructions for Authors and the Editor Guidelines. Please read the guidelines carefully and refer to the checklist at the end. It is also important that you use the attached licence-to-publish agreement. None of the other agreements are applicable here. If possible, a partially completed agreement should be made available to the authors, to be completed and signed by the corresponding author of each paper, on behalf of all of the authors of that paper. The corresponding author, who must match the corresponding author marked on the paper, must have the full right, power, and authority to sign the agreement on behalf of all of the authors of a particular paper, and accepts responsibility for releasing this material on their behalf. Once the files have been sent to Springer, changes relating to the authorship of the papers cannot be made.

As to the timeline, we need 8 – 10 weeks upon receipt of your complete set of electronic files to publish the proceedings. Rapid dissemination of the scientific work presented at conferences is one of the advantages of publishing proceedings, so we would encourage you to send the manuscript files as soon as possible. If the authors have been given the opportunity to improve their papers following the event and before sending their final submissions to you, please inform me. This will have an effect on the subtitle of the proceedings.

Information regarding free copies and the prices of additional copies can be found in the attached Conf Terms PDF. If you wish to opt for 4-week free access for the participants via the conference website, kindly inform us when you send us the files of the papers for publication.

Please note that abstracts of keynote talks that are not accompanied by a full paper will be included in the front matter and not in the main part of the proceedings. We ask you to inform the authors of such abstracts. Please refer to Sect. 4.2 of the guidelines for more details regarding paper lengths and the inclusion of short papers.

When you are in the process of collecting the final papers from the authors, please contact anna.kramer@springer.com for a volume number. If you have more than 700 pages, two volume numbers will be needed, and so on. When we send you your volume number, we shall also request some information from you regarding the peer review process and acceptance statistics pertaining to your event. In the long term, we would like to improve review transparency in conference proceedings by making this information available on SpringerLink and CrossRef’s Crossmark.

We’ve attached an example of a front matter. You can see in this our default requirements for details in the Preface and the structure of the Organization. The templates for generating the front matter are available from our website.

Also attached is an Excel file that can be used as a template for the Table of Contents and for listing the corresponding authors.

We would like to draw your attention to Sect. 3.2 of the attached CS Editors Proceedings Guidelines, which refer to the inclusion of Alternative Text. It is not obligatory for authors to provide this, and we understand that it may not be convenient for you to contact them in this way. If no Alternative Text is provided with the papers, our typesetters will generate and insert this text. In such cases, the corresponding authors will have an opportunity to check it during the proofing stage.

Roughly one month after publication, all authors who have included their email addresses in the headers of their papers will receive an email containing a coded personal access link, which will enable them to download the PDF of their paper directly from SpringerLink.

Additionally, all volume editors will be given free access to the entire series for the rest of the year, directly after publication of their proceedings.

Finally, following this acceptance email, FISEE 2026 will be included in our list of Forthcoming Proceedings, which is available from our website.

Volume Editors

By default, for Springer Computer Science proceedings volumes, the volume editors (the people named on the book cover) should be the Program Committee Chairs, and they should also be the people who write the volume Preface. In this way, the Program Committee Chairs take responsibility for the scientific program of the conference, and for decisions on the content of the Springer-published proceedings. Attached to this email, please find a template for the preface and the organization pages.

If you plan any other approach for these proceedings, i.e., if you plan to have volume editors who are not the Program Committee chairs, please contact Ronan Nugent now to explain and to seek approval. Your suggestion will be evaluated, and if approved we will advise you about our related conditions (including how this decision-making structure should be revealed later in the published Preface and Organization pages).
